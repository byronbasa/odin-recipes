Burger Recipe

Description:

A hamburger is a classic, satisfying meal made by seasoning ground beef, shaping it into patties, and cooking them until juicy and flavorful. The patties are served on toasted buns and topped with ingredients like lettuce, tomato, cheese, and condiments. This recipe creates a simple, customizable burger perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients:

  • 1 large egg
  • ½ teaspoon of salt
  • ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 pound ground beef
