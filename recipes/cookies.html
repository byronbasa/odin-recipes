Chocolate Chip Cookies Recipe

Description

Chocolate chip cookies are a classic homemade treat made by mixing butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla with flour and baking soda to form a dough. Chocolate chips are folded in, and spoonfuls of dough are baked until golden brown on the edges and soft in the center. The result is a warm, sweet cookie with a chewy texture and melted chocolate throughout.

Ingredients:

  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla
  • Baking Soda
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Flour
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Nuts (optional)

Steps:

  1. Beat the butter and sugars, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  2. Dissolve the baking soda in hot water and add to the mixture.
  3. Stir in the flour, chocolate chips, and walnuts.
  4. Drop dough onto a prepared baking sheet.
  5. Bake until the edges are golden brown.
